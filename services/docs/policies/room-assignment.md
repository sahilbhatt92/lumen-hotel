# Room assignment

Assignment puts a room number on a reservation. It does not, by itself, mean the guest has arrived.

## What can be assigned

Assign a vacant or inspected room that is not out of order. Occupied rooms stay with the guest who is in house. A dirty room waits for housekeeping. A future reservation may hold a vacant room; the room remains unoccupied until check-in.

## Moving a guest

A confirmed reservation can move to another assignable room before arrival. An in-house guest can move only onto a vacant or inspected room. The room they leave becomes dirty.

## Unassigned arrivals

An arrival without a room is not ready for check-in. Choose a clean room, assign it, and only then check the guest in.
