# Staff requests

A staff request is how one watch leaves work for another. It is not a reservation and it does not change who is in house.

## What belongs in a request

Log a request for housekeeping, maintenance, an amenity, or a front-desk follow-up. Name the room when a room is known. Write a summary a colleague can act on without hearing the original conversation.

## Movement

A new request is open. The colleague who picks it up marks it in progress. The colleague who finishes it marks it done. Done requests stay visible through the end of the business date so the next watch can read them.

## What not to log

Do not log a check-in, a cancellation, or a room assignment as a staff request. Those are reservation operations and they have their own result on the stay.
