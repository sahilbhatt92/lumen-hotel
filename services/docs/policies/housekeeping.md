# Housekeeping

Housekeeping owns the condition of the room. The desk owns the promise made to the guest.

## Departure clean

A departure room is marked dirty at check-out. The attendant strips linen, resets amenities, and checks the balcony latch. The room returns to inspected only after a supervisor walk-through. Front desk does not check a new guest into a dirty room.

## Stayover service

An occupied room is serviced on request, or on every second day of the stay if the guest does not decline service. Extra pillows, cribs, and rollaways are staff requests. They do not change the room status.

## Out of order

Engineering can take a room out of order. That room leaves the assignable inventory until it is marked vacant again. Do not promise an out-of-order room to an arrival, even if the board still shows it on the floor.
