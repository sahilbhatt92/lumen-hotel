# Late checkout

Checkout at Harbor House is a morning event. The house needs the rooms back for the afternoon arrivals.

## Standard departure

Checkout is 11:00 on the departure date. At check-out the reservation is checked out and the room becomes dirty for a departure clean.

## Until 1:00 pm

A harbor or lumen guest may be offered 1:00 pm when the next arrival for that room is not the same day, or when that arrival is after 4:00 pm. Front desk logs a front-desk request and tells housekeeping the room will be late. A member guest is offered 1:00 pm only when the room has no same-day arrival.

## After 1:00 pm

A departure later than 1:00 pm is an extra night when the room is needed, or a manager exception when it is not. The desk does not grant an open-ended checkout.
