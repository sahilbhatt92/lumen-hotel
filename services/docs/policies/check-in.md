# Check-in

Check-in is a front-desk operation. It is not a staff request and it is not a room assignment by itself.

## When a guest may check in

Check-in is for a confirmed reservation whose arrival date is the business date. The room on the reservation must already be assigned. The room status must be vacant or inspected. A dirty room, an occupied room, or a room that is out of order stops check-in until housekeeping or engineering releases it.

An arrival with no room assigned is not ready. Assign a clean room first, then check the guest in.

## Identification

Ask for a government photo identification and match the name to the reservation. Confirm that the identification was seen. Do not copy the document number into the desk notes. If the name differs from the reservation, stop and call a manager before issuing keys.

## Keys and status

After identification, mark the reservation in house and the room occupied. Say the departure date and the Harbor House checkout time of 11:00. If the guest asks for something the room does not already have, log a staff request instead of changing the room status.

## Early arrivals

A guest who arrives before the arrival date is not checked in on this desk. Offer to hold bags and confirm the room will be ready on the arrival date.
