# Cancellation and deposits

Harbor House takes a first-night deposit when a reservation is confirmed. The deposit is the posted rate for the arrival night, not a flat fee.

## Fee-free window

A confirmed reservation can be cancelled without a fee when the business date is at least two nights before the arrival date. The guest is released from the rest of the stay, and the deposit goes back to the original method. Staff say the release plainly and do not offer a different rate to keep the booking.

## Inside the window

When arrival is tomorrow or today, a cancellation forfeits the first night. Later nights are released. Record the forfeiture against the reservation and do not debate the amount at the desk. The night kept is the rate already on the stay.

## No-show

A confirmed arrival with no check-in and no message by 11:00 pm on the arrival date is a no-show. The first night is kept. Do not mark a no-show earlier than that.

## What is not a cancellation

Shortening a stay after the guest is in house is a departure change. It is not handled with this policy. A room move is an assignment, not a cancellation.
